# Commercial use of Suite Builder

Suite Builder is source-available, not open source. Under the Suite Builder Noncommercial License
1.0 (see `LICENSE`) anyone may read the code and use it for personal, noncommercial purposes:
study, experiments, hobby projects. Charities and schools may also use it under those terms.

**Any business use needs a commercial license from the author,** for example:

- running Suite Builder at work, for an employer or a client,
- building it into a product, service or internal tool,
- using it to deliver paid consulting or testing work.

**Any government use needs a license from the author too,** commercial or not: governments,
their agencies, militaries, police, intelligence and security services, and anyone working on
their behalf. The author decides case by case and will not license Suite Builder to governments
whose leaders have authorized human rights violations.

Test projects that Suite Builder has generated under a valid license are a separate matter: the
person or organization they were generated for may keep and use them freely (see
`GENERATED-OUTPUT.md`). That permission does not cover projects generated through use no license
covers; the author reserves all rights in those.

## Kinds of commercial license

- **Employment license.** For an organization that employs the author. The organization may use
  Suite Builder internally for as long as the author works there. When the employment ends, for
  any reason, the license ends too, unless the organization buys a perpetual internal-use license
  at a price agreed in advance. A draft agreement is available on request.
- **Organization license.** For anyone else, on terms agreed with the author.
